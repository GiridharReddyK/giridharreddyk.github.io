  2. IEEE

    Design of Ground Station Antennas for Tracking of LoRa-Based Satellites at 433 MHz Amateur Frequencies

    K. Vasanth, K. Giridhar Reddy, V. Bala Sai Datta, K. Suman

    Proc. 2025 IEEE International Students' Conference on Electrical, Electronics and Computer Science (SCEECS 2025), MANIT Bhopal, 18–19 January 2025 · IEEE Xplore, 2 April 2025 Best Paper Award

    IEEE Xplore

    We propose a real-time quarter-wave (¼λ) ground-plane antenna at 433 MHz for tracking LoRa satellites. Several ground-station antennas were designed and simulated to compare their characteristics, then built and connected to a ground station made of an ESP module and a LoRa module, linked over the Internet of Things to the VayuVani and TinyGS networks. The quarter-wave ground-plane antenna was better at receiving packets from LoRa satellites: it received telemetry from slant ranges of around 3,101 km and logged more than 4,000 telemetry packets.
